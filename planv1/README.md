## About the design bundle

A dated snapshot, exported 2026-09-01, from before any of this was implemented. Two things to
know when reading it:

- `Extraction Level Set.dc.html` is marked "concept · none of this is built yet", and
  `github.md` says the hosted multi-user mode does not exist in the repo. **Both statements
  were true when the bundle was exported and are no longer true** — group mode is built. Read
  them as a record of the starting point, not as current status.
- The concept proposes more than was built. Comments and +1s, the coverage map UI, the scope
  ledger, the three registers (questions, assumptions, definitions), the two-workshop
  engagement log and facilitator merge-of-duplicates are all deliberately out of scope; see
  the "Deferred" section of `level-set-plan-v2.md`.

`docs/screenshots/` are of the shipped single-user app and remain accurate.
